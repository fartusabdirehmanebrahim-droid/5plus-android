Close your 5PLUS account

To ask for your 5PLUS account to be closed and your personal information deleted:

  1. Open the 5PLUS app and go to Account → Technical support.
  2. Send your request from the phone number registered on the account.
  3. Any money left in your wallet or earnings, and any open orders, must be settled first.

Once your request is confirmed, your account is closed and your personal details are removed. Some records may be kept where the law requires or allows it.

See the privacy policy.
