Delete your 5PLUS account

This is how to delete an account in the 5PLUS app (by 5PLUS / Sarrif).

In the app

  1. Send out any money left in your 5PLUS wallet and withdraw your earnings.
  2. Finish or cancel any open orders.
  3. Open Account and press Delete my account at the bottom.
  4. Enter your 4-digit PIN. Your account is deleted straight away.

If you can't open the app

Contact 5PLUS support on WhatsApp (the number is in the app under Account → Technical support) from the phone number of the account and ask for it to be deleted. We delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your name, phone number, PIN, wallet numbers, face photo, shop details and products (merchants), and your notification token. You can't log in again; the phone number can be used for a new account.
  • Kept: records of transfers, orders and payments, with your name removed, because financial records must be kept by law. Chat messages stay visible to the person you chatted with, shown as "Deleted account".

See also the privacy policy.
