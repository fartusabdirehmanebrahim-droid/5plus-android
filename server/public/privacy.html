5PLUS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Applies to the 5PLUS app for Android and the website fiveplus.onrender.com

5PLUS (also called Sarrif) lets people send money between mobile wallets, shop in Suuq, book services and pay safely. This page explains what information we keep, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, phone number and 4-digit PIN. The PIN is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; nobody at 5PLUS can read it.
  • Wallet numbers: the mobile money numbers you add (for example ZAAD, eDahab, Soltelco, Premier Wallet), so transfers and payments go to the right place.
  • Face photo (optional): if you add one at registration, only the administration sees it, to check who owns an account.
  • Merchants: the registration details you give (full name, date of birth, country, city, business name, category, address, merchant numbers) and the products, photos and videos you post.
  • Orders and payments: what you buy or sell, amounts, payment method, the USSD code dialled and the status of each order, plus wallet and earnings history.
  • Chats and calls: messages and photos you send in chats. Voice calls go directly between the two phones over the internet; we don't record them.
  • Device: a notification token so we can send you order and chat alerts, and a login cookie so you stay logged in.

Phone permissions the app uses

  • Phone calls: to dial the USSD payment code (for example *883*…#) after you enter your PIN, so you don't have to type it. The app doesn't read your call history or contacts.
  • Camera and photos: only when you take or choose a photo (face photo, product photos, chat photos).
  • Microphone: only during a call you start or answer in chat.
  • Notifications: to alert you about orders, payments and messages.

How we use it

To run your account, process transfers and orders, show sellers who ordered from them, let admins check payments and settle problems, pay earnings, send you alerts, prevent fraud, and meet legal duties.

Who sees it

  • The other side of an order: a seller sees the buyer's name and phone number for that order; a buyer sees the shop's details.
  • 5PLUS administrators: to confirm payments, release held money, review products and help with support.
  • Service providers that run 5PLUS for us: hosting (Render), database (Supabase) and push notifications (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Mobile money operators receive the payment you make when your phone dials their USSD code.

We don't sell your information and we don't use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while you use it. When you delete it, your name, phone number, wallet numbers, face photo, shop details and notification token are removed. Records of money that moved (transfers, orders, payments) are kept with your name removed, because financial records must be kept by law.

Delete your account

In the app: Account → Delete my account, then enter your PIN. First send out any money left in your wallet or earnings and finish any open orders. If you can't open the app, ask our support team (see below) from the phone number of the account. Details: how to delete your 5PLUS account.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), PINs are hashed, and every payment needs your PIN.

Children

5PLUS is not meant for children under 18.

Contact

Use Account → Technical support in the app to reach our team on WhatsApp. We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
