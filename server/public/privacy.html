5PLUS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Applies to the 5PLUS app and website

This policy explains what information 5PLUS ("we", "us") collects when you use the 5PLUS app and website (the "Service"), how it is used and the choices you have. By using the Service you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, phone number and PIN.
  • Payment information: the mobile wallet numbers you give us, and the history of your transfers, orders and payments.
  • Merchant information: the business and identity details you give when you register as a merchant, and the products, photos and videos you post.
  • Verification photo (optional): a photo you may add so we can confirm who owns an account.
  • Content you send: chat messages, photos and other content you share with other users.
  • Device and usage information: information needed to keep you logged in, send you notifications and keep the Service working and safe.

Phone permissions

The app may ask for permission to: make phone calls (to start the payment codes you confirm), use the camera and photos (when you choose to add a picture), use the microphone (during calls you make or answer in the app) and show notifications. You can turn these permissions off in your phone settings; some features may then not work.

How we use information

  • To provide, operate and improve the Service.
  • To process transfers, orders and payments that you start.
  • To let buyers and sellers complete orders with each other.
  • To send you notifications about your account and activity.
  • To verify accounts, prevent fraud and misuse, and settle disputes.
  • To meet legal obligations and protect the rights and safety of our users and of 5PLUS.

How information is shared

  • With other users as needed to complete an order (for example, a seller receives the buyer's name and phone number for that order).
  • With service providers that help us run the Service, under obligations to protect it.
  • With mobile money and payment operators that you choose to pay with.
  • When required by law, or when needed to prevent fraud, protect our users or protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information.

Keeping your information

We keep information for as long as your account is active and afterwards as long as needed for legal, accounting, dispute and fraud-prevention purposes.

Your responsibilities

  • Keep your PIN and phone secure and do not share them. You are responsible for all activity under your account.
  • Check that the phone and wallet numbers, amounts and details you enter are correct before you confirm.
  • Merchants are responsible for their products, services, prices and descriptions.

Third-party services

Payments are made through mobile money operators and other third parties, and some support may be given through outside apps. Those services have their own terms and privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Limitation of liability

The Service is provided "as is" and "as available". To the fullest extent permitted by law, 5PLUS is not liable for any loss or damage arising from: a PIN or phone that was shared, lost or stolen; incorrect numbers or details entered by a user; delays, failures or errors of mobile networks, payment operators or other third parties; the products, services or conduct of merchants or other users; or any indirect or consequential loss.

Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18.

Your choices

You can review your account details in the app. To ask for your account to be closed or your information to be deleted, contact our support team from the phone number registered on the account. Some information may be kept where the law requires or allows it.
